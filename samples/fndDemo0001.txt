Pricing a developer tool starts with who pays. If the buyer is the team lead, price per seat; if the buyer is the company, price per workspace.

Acme Queue costs 20 dollars per month for the team plan, and that is a good example of a simple flat price that is easy to explain.

According to our survey, 40 percent of developers prefer usage based pricing, but a flat price converts better for small teams.

You should start with a flat price, talk to ten customers, and only then add usage tiers. Avoid discounts in the first month because they hide the real signal.

Monetization is the process of turning product value into revenue, and it is different from marketing.

Make sure the free plan has a clear limit so users understand when they should upgrade.
